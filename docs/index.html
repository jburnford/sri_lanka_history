PROVENANCE

Sources & methods

The atlas brings together maps, census volumes, administrative records, and present-day geographic references.

Census

Each point in a census series represents a specific source. Compare readings by source and year, including provisional and retrospective counts. Census geometries are approximate, and some towns use present-day anchor points.

Transport

Geometry dates describe the mapped or reconstructed feature. The dated records document openings, works, and other events. Candidate alignments and schematic traces carry review notes.
