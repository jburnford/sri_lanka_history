# Simon Casie Chitty’s gazetteer and the Ceylon HGIS

## Edition and available scans

Simon Casie Chitty, Modliar, *The Ceylon Gazetteer*, Cotta Church Mission Press, Ceylon, **1834**. The 1834 title page is visible in the original scan. The user's Internet Archive OCR comes from a **newly typeset New Delhi Navrang reprint catalogued in 1989**, with a foreword by Mervyn St. S. Casie Chetty. Its imprint notes first publication in 1834 and its foreword explains that the text was recomposed. Internet Archive's 1834 metadata describes the underlying work.
