# Census of Ceylon, 1881 and 1891

Lionel Lee’s *Census of Ceylon, 1891*, Volume II, *Detailed Tables of Area and Population*, was printed by George J. A. Skeen in Colombo in 1892. The project’s source scan is Internet Archive item `wbsl.4536`. [Volume II, PDF](https://archive.org/download/wbsl.4536/4536.pdf)

- `census_1891_comparison.tsv` transcribes the six enumerated population columns in Table III, pp. 12–15 (PDF pp. 17–20). The columns are persons, males and females for each census. The table excludes the military and shipping. Coverage includes Ceylon, all nine province totals, all 22 source district rows, and 17 municipality, local board and town-scope rows. Two Negombo rows correspond to its two different scopes. [Table III](https://archive.org/download/wbsl.4536/4536.pdf#page=17)
- `census_1891_area_density.tsv` transcribes area in square miles and population per square mile from Table II, p. 11 (PDF p. 16). The table prints Colombo Municipality’s area as 9 9/20 square miles exclusive of Colombo Lake. Chilaw’s area is obscured in the scan. Its density of 241 is legible. [Table II](https://archive.org/download/wbsl.4536/4536.pdf#page=16)

The census reference dates are 17 February 1881 and 26 February 1891. Table III uses the 1891 headings for the comparative series. The 1881 counts for Uva and Sabaragamuwa describe the territories represented by those headings. Each district row retains its published historical scope and name, with capitalization standardized and ditto marks expanded.

The comparison distinguishes Colombo Municipality, Colombo District exclusive of the municipality, and Negombo District. It also distinguishes Vavuniya and Mullaitivu districts. It names Nuwarakaláwiya District, including Tamankaduwa, in North-Central Province. The join to historical Anuradhapura uses successor context. The 1946 district map requires separate interpretation for these earlier counts. The 1891 scan shows Negombo’s population as 9,522 on its 1881 Local Board limits and 18,933 on its contemporary limits. The older limits have their own feature ID. Table III footnotes specify scope changes at Ratnapura, Kurunegala, Chilaw, Matale and Batticaloa. Jaffna Division is present in Table III and covers more than the town. It is outside this town extraction.

Every transcribed persons total equals its male and female components. For each census, all nine province totals sum to the published national total. In each province the component district counts sum to the printed total, including Colombo Municipality as its own component of Western Province.
