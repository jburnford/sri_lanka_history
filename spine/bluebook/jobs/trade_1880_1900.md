## Input

`tables/<key>.jsonl.gz` — one printed table per line, parsed from OCR. Fields: `id` (e.g. `bb1896:p529:t1` =
volume 1896, PDF page 529, table 1), `year`, `pdf_page`, `printed_page`, `headings`, `header_rows`, `n_cols`,
`rows` (rows of cell strings; row and column spans are already expanded, so a spanned cell repeats).
The OCR is good but not perfect: digits are sometimes wrong, and cells are sometimes shifted one column left
or right or merged. Never correct a figure. Never guess where a figure belongs: if a row cannot be read
safely, write it to the skipped file with a reason.

## The printed layout

One block per page. The ledger tables have a first header cell `Articles.` and a second starting
`Countries whence imported` (imports) or `Countries to which exported` (exports).

Imports, typically 9 columns (look at `bb1896:p529:t1`):

    Articles. | Countries whence imported. | Total quantities imported | Quantities entered for home consumption |
    Value in currency: of total imports (Rs. | c.) | Duty: gross amount received (Rs. | c.) | rate, and when imposed

Exports, typically 12 columns (look at `bb1896:p575:t1`):

    Articles. | Countries to which exported. | Quantities: produce and manufactures of the Colony | British, foreign
    and other colonial produce | total | Value in currency: produce of the Colony (Rs. | c.) | other produce
    (Rs. | c.) | average price fixed for duty | duty

Rows:

    Confectionery | United Kingdom ... | 837 0 7 | 795 1 22 | 36108 | 83 | 2328 | 90 |      article + first country
                  | Br. Col.           |                                                     country-group heading
                  | Australia ...      | 8 2 15  | 8 2 15   | 1630  | 75 | 106  | 3  |
                  | For. Coun.         |
                  | France ...         | 1 0 0   | 1 0 0    | 138   | 64 | 9    | 1  |
                  |                    | 1833 1 22 | 1791 3 9 | 53807 | 30 | ...            unlabelled row = the article's total

- The article is named once, in the first column, on the row of its first country (the OCR sometimes repeats
  it down the column, and sometimes puts it on a row of its own). Headings that span the whole row
  ("Articles of food and drink") are classes or groups, not articles.
- A weight is printed as hundredweights, quarters and pounds: `837 0 7` is 837 cwt. 0 qr. 7 lb. The unit
  ("cwt.", "No.", "gallons", "pkgs.", "yds.") is printed at the head of the article's figures, on the first
  row or a row of its own, or as a prefix in the cell ("cwts. 200 3 15"); `"` or `do.` means the same as above.
  The OCR sometimes splits cwt and "qr lb" into two cells (`419` | `1 11`): then join them.
- Money is rupees and cents, usually in two cells (`36108` | `83`), sometimes one (`522 64`).
- `—`, `...`, blank mean no figure.

## Output

`extracted/trade_1880_1900.tsv.gz` (gzip, tab-separated, one row per printed figure), header exactly:

    table_id year_volume pdf_page printed_page flow class_as_printed group_as_printed article_as_printed country_group_as_printed country_as_printed unit_as_printed measure value_as_printed value row_kind note

- `flow`: import / export. `row_kind`: country / article_total / subtotal.
- `measure`: imports `quantity_total`, `quantity_home_consumption`, `value_total_rs`, `duty_rs`;
  exports `quantity_ceylon_produce`, `quantity_other_produce`, `quantity_total`, `value_ceylon_produce_rs`,
  `value_other_produce_rs`, `value_total_rs` (only where printed). Do not output the average price or the rate.
- `value_as_printed`: the cell text as printed (for money `36108 83`; for a weight `837 0 7`).
- `value`: a plain decimal number, or empty for no figure. Money: rupees with cents as two decimals
  (36108.83). Weights in cwt-qr-lb: decimal hundredweights (cwt + qr/4 + lb/112) to four decimals, and put
  `cwt qr lb` in `note`. Other quantities: the number.
- `note`: always `source row N` (zero-based row in the table), plus anything a reader must know
  (unit carried from above; cells joined; article heading on the row above).
- `country_group_as_printed`: `Br. Col.` or `For. Coun.` as printed; empty for United Kingdom.

`extracted/trade_1880_1900_skipped.tsv`: table_id, row_index, cells (JSON), reason.

`extracted/trade_1880_1900_checks.tsv`: one row per article and measure where the article's total row does
not equal the sum of its country rows (compare in pounds for weights and in cents for money, exactly):
year_volume, flow, article_as_printed, article_start_table, total_table_id, total_row_index, measure,
printed_total, country_sum, difference.
